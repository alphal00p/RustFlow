# Remaining four-loop reference constructions

No amplitude reference values have been generated by this investigation. The
two raised occupied-line targets below remain missing mandatory references.
All formulas are validation-only; they must not become production reduction
identities, boundary conditions or fit constraints.

The exact input files are `examples/finite_density/five_vertex_eight_edge.json`
and `examples/finite_density/triangular_prism.json`. Both supplemental targets
retain the original shifted-Euclidean polynomial
`N=(P1.P2)^2+(P1.P3)*(P2.P4)`. The eight-edge target raises slot 0 (`P1²`); the
prism raises slot 1 (`P2²`). Independent squared masses must be introduced before
applying the corresponding `-d/dm²`, including every occupied support and every
uncut occurrence of that line.

## Eight-edge graph: an actual interior pole

The charged slots are `{0,2,4,6,7}`, all with positive routed chemical shift.
The complete connected decomposition has 5 one-cut, 10 two-cut, 10 three-cut
and 4 four-cut sectors. The positive-eta spacelike-channel certificate covers
only 12 of these 29 sectors.

In the maximal cut `[0,2,4,7]`, define future massless momenta

```text
q0=P1, q2=P3, q4=P1-P4, q7=P1-P2.
P4=q0-q4, P2=q0-q7, Q6=q2+q4-q0.
```

The three neutral Euclidean denominators are pairwise spacelike differences.
The remaining charged denominator is `rho6=-(q2+q4-q0)_M²`. It has an interior
on-shell surface, not only a soft or collinear endpoint. For example, in
Minkowski coordinates `(E,x,y,z)` take

```text
q0=(1/2,1/2,0,0), q2=(1/2,0,0,1/2),
q4=(1/2,0,0,-1/2), q7=(1/4,0,1/4,0).
```

Then `Q6=(1/2,-1/2,0,0)` is future lightlike. Every charged energy is strictly
inside `0<E<mu=1`, while `(rho1,rho3,rho5)=(1/4,1/2,1/4)` are nonzero. The
original I91 numerator is `1/16`, and the supplemental polynomial is `-3/64`.
Neither removes this pole. A second rational witness with unequal shell energies
is retained in the diagnostic artifact below. At both points the derivative of
Delta with respect to the original spatial coordinate `p1_x`, holding the other
loop vectors fixed, equals one. The pole surface is transverse there.

The four maximal cuts contain slot 7 and omit respectively slots `6,4,2,0` from
the remaining charged circuit. Put

```text
Delta=E0+E6-E2-E4, s_missing=(+1,-1,-1,+1).
rho_missing=s_missing*2*E_missing*Delta-Delta².
```

At Delta=0, their neutral denominators, original numerator and product of all
five shell Jacobians coincide in common original spatial coordinates. On this
open occupation cell the simple-base residues are proportional to
`(+1,-1,-1,+1)` and sum to zero. At the first witness the I91 pole residues are
`(4,-4,-4,4)`; the supplemental simple-base residues are `(-3,3,3,-3)`.
Raising slot 0 requires differentiating the complete jointly regulated identity,
including the term where slot 0 is uncut. Freezing an on-shell numerator or
differentiating only those terms where slot 0 was cut does not preserve it.

### Why an independent denominator PV is insufficient

For a fixed real-shell quartet, assigning the same boundary `rho-i0` to each
uncut denominator yields locally

```text
1/(s*2E*Delta-i0) = s/(2E)*PV(1/Delta)
                    + i*pi/(2E)*delta(Delta).
```

Thus its PV pole pieces cancel, but its imaginary contact pieces have the same
sign. Their summed coefficients at the first witness are `16*i*pi` for I91 and
`-12*i*pi` for the supplemental simple base, multiplying `delta(Delta)` and
the remaining spatial measure. The opposite uniform boundary reverses these
imaginary signs. Merely observing cancellation of real poles misses this issue.

This is a **quartet-level diagnostic**. Lower-cut virtual amplitudes can have
discontinuity or contact terms on the same locus. The calculation does not prove
that the complete 29-cut sum with any given boundary prescription is imaginary,
nor that no consistent separate-cut integration exists. Those other terms must
be included in a complete prescription comparison.

For contrast, keep independent line-pole energies complex,
`E_j -> E_j-i*gamma_j`, before decomposing the original regularized integrand.
To leading order every member then inherits the same circuit displacement

```text
Delta -> Delta-i*H, H=gamma0+gamma6-gamma2-gamma4,
rho_missing -> s_missing*2E_missing*(Delta-i*H).
```

Its leading pole and contact contributions cancel together. Here gamma_j are
independent displacements of propagator poles, for example induced by regulated
line mass squares. They are not a common shift of routed loop contours: routing
such a shift would enforce H=0 identically. This algebraic observation supplies
a necessary consistency check; it is not a prescription for theta functions of
complex energies or a proof of equivalence to the thermal contour.

The original request fixes a common regulated thermal definition. Section 4 and
Appendix C of the [cutting-rules paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1609.04339v2)
distinguish original contour singularities from artificial singularities created
by a cut decomposition. The [finite-density IBP derivation](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.05427v2)
also retains the distributional terms produced by raised poles. These results
do not provide a denominator-by-denominator prescription for this new graph.

An independent E8 reference must therefore either combine temporal residues on
common spatial coordinates before removing their regulator, or evaluate complete
cut amplitudes with one derived common regulator and prove that summation and
integration commute with its removal. The order of line-pole regulator removal,
T->0, auxiliary/physical mass limits, cut summation and Laurent expansion must
be explicit. Ordinary real radial quadrature of individual singular cut kernels
is not presently justified. Timelike channels alone would not prove this: the
two-cut `[0,6]` amplitude is a single-invariant timelike form factor, which could
instead be defined by a coherent complex branch continuation.

One candidate validation-only route is to perform all four temporal integrations
in a common thermal/residue representation, group the intersecting pole loci,
then construct UV and physical IR subtractions on the combined integrand.
The primary [dense-LTD four-loop study](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.17921v2)
describes combining temporal integrations and local subtraction for independent
numerical integration. Its published examples do not establish ten-digit
precision for these targets. A Monte Carlo implementation alone supplies no such
precision guarantee, and no implementation of this reference route is claimed.

## Prism: a positive parameter representation is available

The only charged slots are `{1,5,7}`. They give three one-cut sectors, three
two-cut sectors and one three-cut sector. Single-cut physical massless virtual
amplitudes have only one lightlike external invariant and are scaleless under
the common dimensional/analytic prescription. For a raised line, a valid
mass-removal domain must precede that conclusion; setting a mass to zero in an
already divergent massive Laurent series is insufficient.

In the maximal cut choose

```text
q1=P2, q2=P2-P4, q3=P2-P1,
P4=q1-q2, P1=q1-q3, P3=K,
hij=-(qi-qj)_M²=2*ri*rj*(1-zij) >= 0.
```

The six uncut neutral factors are three constants `h12*h13*h23` and the triangle
`K²*(K-P4)²*(K-P1)²`. With x0+x1+x2=1 its Euclidean parameter polynomial is

```text
F=x0*x1*h12+x0*x2*h13+x1*x2*h23,
K_mean=x1*P4+x2*P1,
nu=3-D/2, C_D=(4*pi)^(-D/2)*Gamma(nu).
```

The exact Gaussian numerator kernels are

```text
Nbar_I115=(x1*h12+x2*h13)^3/8,
Nbar_supplement_base=h13²/4
  +(h12/2)*[x1*(h13+h12-h23)/2+x2*h13].
```

The metric terms in the cubic numerator vanish because q1²=0; the supplemental
kernel is at most linear in K. The complete simple-base three-cut contribution
is

```text
- integral product_i[dWi] * C_D * integral_simplex dx
      Nbar/[h12*h13*h23 * F^nu],
dWi=A_d*ri^(d-2)*dri/2, 0<ri<mu, d=D-1,
```

with normalized angular averages. The minus sign is the product of three
Euclidean occupied corrections. This is an independent direct parameter
representation, not an AMF boundary.

For the raised supplemental target give q1 an independent squared mass a. Keep
r1 fixed, replace `E1=sqrt(r1²+a)`, and use

```text
h1j(a)=-a+2*E1*rj-2*r1*rj*z1j,
Nbar(a)=(h13-a)²/4
 +(h12-a)/2 * [x1*(h13+h12-h23)/2+x2*h13].
```

Apply `-d/da` to the entire negative three-cut expression, including
`theta(mu-E1)/(2E1)`, before mass removal. If
`K(a)=C_D*Nbar(a)/(h12*h13*h23*F^nu)`, the massless bulk is
`product_i dWi * [K_a-K/(2*r1²)]`. The upper surface is negative and contains
`-A_d*mu^(d-3)/4` times the remaining two occupied measures and `K` at r1=mu.
The explicit a-dependence in Nbar is necessary even though the original momentum
numerator is held fixed.

An independent audit confirms these bulk and surface signs. The derivative
remains a kernel under the common analytic prescription: near collinearity,
`h1j(a)` can become negative for arbitrarily small positive a when rj<r1.
The positive-eta mass-neighborhood certificate therefore does not justify
commuting this derivative with the eta-to-zero limit.

There is a concrete obstruction to using an unregulated real-D convergence
strip for this individual raised sector. Take q1 parallel to q2, r1!=r2,
q3 generic, and a point in the interior of the triangle simplex. Then
`h12 ~ theta^2`, `Nbar|h12=0=h13^2/4 != 0`, `F>0`, and
`d_a h12|a=0=-1+r2/r1 != 0`. Consequently K_a has a nonzero
`h12^(-2)` term. The angular measure `theta^(D-3) dtheta` makes its
absolute local integral converge only for Re D>6, while the scalar virtual
triangle already requires Re D<6 in the ultraviolet. The absolute condition
for the unsimplified linear numerator is stricter still. Thus this sector
needs analytic-index regulation, explicit subtraction, or proved cancellation
with the other cut sectors before the massless raised endpoint can be used.
This is an analytic-continuation issue in addition to the numerical integration
cost; no positive-eta certificate resolves it by itself.

All three compact angular variables can be represented by z12,z13,w in [-1,1],
with `z23=z12*z13+sqrt(1-z12²)*sqrt(1-z13²)*w`. Their normalized weights are
proportional to
`(1-z12²)^((d-3)/2)*(1-z13²)^((d-3)/2)*(1-w²)^((d-4)/2)`.
For Re d>2 this is an ordinary angular integral. A sector decomposition must
still resolve pairwise and simultaneous collinear boundaries of F and hij.

Homogeneity removes one radial integration exactly. Partition according to the
largest radius and put `(r_j,r_k,r_l)=R*(1,x,y)`. The supplemental simple-base
overall radial integral is `mu^(4D-14)/(4D-14)`. Its raised bulk is
`mu^(4D-16)/(4D-16)` and its Fermi-surface term has the same mass dimension.
The apparent global soft pole at D=4 cannot be removed by ordinary quadrature;
this exact radial integration exposes it before Laurent extraction. These
factorizations leave seven compact/triangle-parameter variables. They do not
resolve overlapping soft/collinear subregions on their own.

### The two-cut coefficient problem

For the representative pair `[1,5]`, put `q=P2`, `p=P4=q1-q2`, `p²=h>0`,
`q²=(q-p)²=0` in Euclidean invariant notation. The uncut amplitude consists of
the external `1/h` and six two-loop denominators

```text
K², L², (K-p)², (L-p)², (K-q)², (K-L)².
```

Using parameters a,b,c,d,e,f in that order, define

```text
A=a+c+e+f, B=b+d+f, U=A*B-f²,
Phi=a*b*c+a*c*d+a*c*f+b*c*f+a*b*d
     +b*c*d+b*d*e+b*d*f+a*d*f,
F=h*Phi.
```

Both U and Phi are positive in the parameter interior. Gaussian means and
covariances generate the original polynomial without fitting to any reference
answer. A first off-shell derivative for the raised cut uses `q²=-m²` and
`2*q.p=h-m²`, giving

```text
F=h*Phi-m²*Psi,
Psi=e*(a*b+a*d+a*f+b*f).
```

It must be differentiated together with the Gaussian numerator and occupied
measure. The resulting coefficients are five-dimensional parameter integrals
after projectivization. Homogeneity reduces their compact integrations to beta
integrals of a power of h once the off-shell expansion is justified. The other
two pairs require their own exact routing/numerator maps, even when the scalar
topology is isomorphic.

The exact diagnostic also verifies `F=h*Phi+q²*Psi` directly against Gaussian
completion of the six denominators at independent rational parameters, including
the off-shell value needed for mass differentiation. This check does not
integrate the resulting coefficient functions.

This yields a concrete reference-generation plan: generate the finite set of
Gaussian tensor and mass-derivative parameter integrals; construct exact sector
UV/IR subtractions for U, Phi and the triple-cut F; integrate the exposed scale
powers analytically; then evaluate the finite Laurent coefficient integrands
with independent arbitrary-precision quadrature/order refinements. Complete
cut assembly and independent precision/node/sector checks are required before
claiming twelve reliable reference digits. The current isolated kernels do not
establish attainability of that precision or provide any Laurent values.
