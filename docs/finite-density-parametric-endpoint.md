# Positive parametric endpoint queries

The additive `finite_density::parametric_endpoint` module constructs exact support-scoped evidence. It does not extend `MasslessFlowEvidence`, certify a thermal contour, or evaluate an integral. The native HEPKit `IntegralFamily::symanzik` owner supplies U and F for the actual oriented occupied routing and each positive virtual support. The query verifies F=eta U sum(alpha)+h V and nonnegative rational U,V coefficients. Pure external factors must be nonzero spacelike transfers and are recorded separately.

The complete coordinate-sector tree retains all projective orders, every blowup pivot, the original parameter maps, eta's monomial map, and the exact U/F valuations. Missing charts, negative dimension slopes, nonregular zero-dimension slopes, and budget exhaustion are explicit failures. Native reconstruction and exact map replay are distinct checks. The identity binds the input, assigned family, routing, actual active support, positive polynomial coefficients, proof version and complete tree. No partial support coverage is returned as successful.

The independent saved diagnostic in `reports/validation/2026-10-09-finite-density-native-assembly/prism-support-wide-proof` restricts separately derived rational U,V polynomials to all 64 virtual supports for each of three prism two-cut sectors. Each has 12 rank-deficient supports, 23 full-rank supports with V=0, and 7112 positive-unit leaves across the full-rank supports. This is independent mathematical evidence; native query gates and their build provenance are recorded separately. No four-loop numerical result follows from these counts.

## Finite polynomial and mass-jet bounds

For h virtual loops, P positive virtual powers, and a polynomial insertion of momentum rank at most R, Gaussian terms with c covariance contractions have the form

`U^(P-R-(h+1)D/2) F^(hD/2-P+c)`

times polynomials in the parameters and external vector components. Extra nonnegative U powers can be included in that polynomial. No inverse external Gram matrix is introduced. Finite original shell/occupation jets lower the F power by at most their total J; the original numerator remains fixed before differentiation. A representation that already includes a mass derivative must not be charged that derivative a second time.

On a retained chart, with U=z^u U0, F=z^f F0, eta=z^m and measure z^(j-1) dz, a sufficient eta-Mellin numerator is

`j + (P-R)u - (P+J)f + sum_i (n_i-1) a_i + D*(h*f-(h+1)*u)/2`.

Every m>0 nonregular coordinate requires this expression to be strictly positive in a common high-dimensional neighborhood. The query records the integer coefficients before dividing by m. The regular axis must have u=f=j=0, m=1 and all original parameter valuations zero; at most one occurs per chart. Finite numerator and jet shifts cannot turn that axis into a negative integer eta term.

These are bounds for supplied finite P,R,J, not a physical-label admission API. A future sealed adapter must derive R and all virtual/transfer/occupation powers from each actual basis, candidate and target label, validate index roles first, and bind the resulting bounds to the support query.

## Compact extension for arbitrary finite labels: proof under review

At two null occupied external momenta the only scalar invariant is h>=0. Retaining polynomial external tensor structures, each scalar Gaussian coefficient is homogeneous: K_eta(h)=h^lambda G(eta/h), with lambda>=h_virtual*D/2-P-j_virtual. Complete joint-sector Mellin continuation gives a bounded G near zero at a sufficiently high generic D. The positive Schur complement bounds |Schur_12| by sqrt(C11 C22), so V/(U sum(alpha)) is bounded. The UV-meromorphic massive germ therefore bounds the opposite ratio h/eta near zero. Positive intermediate ratios form a compact smooth interval. Together these give |K_eta(h)|<=C(h+eta)^Re(lambda), away from genuine dimensional poles.

The deformed pure-transfer factors are eta+a_i^2 h with nonzero rational a_i, not undeformed h factors. A finite mass/energy jet divides between virtual coefficients and transfer factors. For each Leibniz term, j_virtual+j_transfer<=J, so a sufficient uniform compact condition is

`h_virtual*Re(D)/2 - P - P0 - J > 0`,

where P0 contains only actual positive transfer powers. It is not necessary to charge J independently twice in this combined inequality. Constants may depend on the nonzero a_i. Derivatives of future shell energies cost at most E_i^(-2J_i). The independent radial requirements Re(D)>2+2J_i and angular requirement Re(D)>2 then supply an integrable compact bound; upper surfaces lie at positive chemical endpoints.

For full-rank V=0 supports the virtual coefficients are positive-eta massive monomials, not zero integrals at finite eta. The same inequality makes eta^(hD/2-P-j_virtual)*(eta+a_i^2 h)^(-P0-j_transfer) vanish uniformly. This is an endpoint statement only and must never become a finite-eta source-zero identity. Rank-deficient supports instead have an unrestricted polynomial virtual direction and use the separate dimensional-scaleless proof.

## Thermal and continuation order

The proof requires the already verified graph-channel condition and a fixed positive eta. Arbitrary complex null rescalings do not preserve the real positivity of difference invariants, so the regulator must be specified.

A sufficient candidate is the native Feynman line-mass prescription m_i^2=-i epsilon_i with independent epsilon_i>0, real spatial momenta, and future pole energies q_i^0=omega_i-i gamma_i=sqrt(r_i^2-i epsilon_i). Then omega_i^2-gamma_i^2=r_i^2, omega_i>=gamma_i>=0. The identity

`(omega_i omega_j-gamma_i gamma_j)^2-r_i^2 r_j^2=(omega_i gamma_j-omega_j gamma_i)^2`

implies omega_i omega_j-gamma_i gamma_j>=r_i r_j. Consequently Re[-(q_i-q_j)^2]>=0 and Re(q_i^2)=0. For the certified zero/single/difference channel class, positive two-forest coefficients therefore preserve Re F_eta>=eta U sum(alpha). This argument uses actual same-sign complex line masses, not arbitrary complex null vectors or independent routed loop-contour shifts. Its binding to the precise thermal construction remains part of the final admission review.

Remove these regulators at fixed positive temperature and eta. The bound gamma_i<=sqrt(epsilon_i/2) gives a uniform strip avoiding complex Fermi poles. Also |q_i^0|>=r_i, so inverse-energy factors from any finite shell-mass jet retain the high-D radial bound. Gaussian tensors and finite UV continuation have polynomial large-energy growth, whereas the real part of the pole energy satisfies omega_i>=r_i and the Fermi tails decay exponentially. Lower-energy shell/contact jets still require their separate sufficiently-high-D origin bounds.

Then take the real-Fermi zero-temperature limit and the certified eta endpoint, and meromorphically continue the resulting dimension family. This does not assert arbitrary simultaneous complex-regulator, temperature and eta limits. The all-support query alone does not prove this contour statement. The multipair compact strata of three or more occupied momenta also require a distinct proof; the two-null query does not cover them.

## Several compact vectors with one virtual loop: separate sufficient theorem

This is a proposed extension, not implemented admission. Suppose the active virtual quadratic coefficients are nonzero real rationals a_j. On the positive simplex their Gaussian coefficient A=sum(alpha_j a_j^2) is bounded above and bounded strictly away from zero. Exact native polynomial evidence must show that the null external part is a sum of pair invariants h_ij with nonnegative coefficient polynomials. The eta coefficient after normalization by A is positive and bounded; it is comparable to eta, and need not equal one. If Re(D)/2-P-J>0, all finite virtual Gaussian jets are bounded on bounded external energies after UV meromorphic continuation. Arbitrarily high finite polynomial rank changes constants and finite Gaussian terms, not this conservative lower exponent.

Require every positive external-only factor to be a nonzero multiple of one future difference, giving eta+c_ij h_ij with c_ij>0. In any term after distributing at most J derivatives, let p'_ij denote the resulting positive pair powers and P'=sum(p'_ij)<=P0+J. If P'>0, generalized Hölder with exponents P'/p'_ij bounds the full correlated angular product by a product of individual pair moments of t_ij^(-P'), where h_ij=4E_i E_j t_ij. Independence of pair distances is not assumed. Each marginal is a Beta measure with parameters D/2-1, and is integrable when Re(D)/2-1>P'. If P'=0 there is no singular angular factor and no Hölder division.

This argument holds on an open interval of real dimensions, not just integer dimensions. For k compact directions, use the normalized positive correlation-Gram measure with density proportional to det(G)^((D-k-2)/2), diagonal G_ii=1 and G>0. Taking the conservative condition Re(D)>k+1 makes this measure and its Schur-complement integrals regular. Its two-vector marginal has density (1-z^2)^((D-4)/2). Absolute values in a small complex-D neighborhood are controlled by the corresponding positive measure at Re(D); the normalization is meromorphic and regular in the chosen neighborhood.

The radial density for loop i is bounded by E_i^(D-3-2J_i-sum_j p'_ij). Sufficient fixed finite-label conditions are therefore

- Re(D)/2-P-J>0 for virtual jets;
- Re(D)/2-1>P0+J for all pair-angle products;
- Re(D)-2-2J_i-sum_j p_ij-J>0 for each radial origin;
- Re(D)>k+1 for a positive regular Gram measure.

Higher thermal occupation derivatives are treated by integration by parts in energy, transferring their finite derivative order to the smooth fixed-eta test function before the real-Fermi limit. High-D origin bounds remove lower endpoint terms; upper chemical endpoints are positive. This avoids a false pointwise bound on higher Fermi derivatives as T goes to zero. The required energy derivatives are included in J_i. The same precise line-mass regulator and fixed-temperature removal order described above remain necessary.
