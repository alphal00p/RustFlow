# Continuous dimensional endpoint and the shared projector

This is a mathematical bridge for the reviewed partial-endpoint theorem, not a live projector modification. It does not require evaluating a period or assigning a sign to every individual basis mode.

Let the complete original target be T(eta,D)=sum_i w_i(eta,D) I_i(eta,D), where the weights are exact rational functions and the I_i are the actual solution selected by the integrated boundary. Assume the retained joint analytic family is meromorphic in D and that, on an open high-real-D interval with a small generic complex neighborhood, T has the uniform continuous eta->0 limit proved by the finite J+N/Hölder bound. Also require an actual complete, convergent regular-singular Frobenius representation of this solution on the prescribed branch. A formal indicial list or positivity of a Symanzik polynomial is insufficient.

Choose a smaller generic neighborhood avoiding coefficient poles, indicial branch singularities and accidental resonances. In this neighborhood the exponent branches are analytic and each nonzero rational weight has a fixed integer eta valuation and a convergent Laurent expansion. The reconstructed target is a finite sum of terms eta^(lambda_a(D)+n) (log eta)^j with convergent integer-power coefficient series and finitely many terms at or below any fixed real order. Constants of the physical solution are meromorphic in D there, as follows from the retained analytic integral family and a nonsingular matching basis.

Group exponent branches modulo identically integer differences. This incorporates collisions between columns and integer shifts from eta-pole weights. An exponent branch that depends nontrivially on D remains nonconstant after any integer shift. It cannot coincide identically with the exponent-zero class. Accidental integer coincidences at isolated dimensions are excluded while proving the identity, and dealt with afterward by meromorphic continuation.

For a fixed generic real D, the finite power/log asymptotic system is linearly independent after those classes are grouped. Continuity forces the combined coefficients of negative-real-power terms, nonconstant pure-oscillation terms, and exponent-zero positive-log terms to vanish. This is a statement about the complete weighted target; separate columns can have singular contributions which cancel. Every remaining term of positive real exponent tends to zero, including finite powers of log eta. The only surviving limit is the combined exponent-zero, log-zero coefficient. A nonconstant-in-D class cannot supply that coefficient on an open D set.

Consequently the physical high-D limit equals the ordinary zero-power/log-zero coefficient of the complete target. Both sides have the retained meromorphic continuation in D, so the equality continues. A varying exponent which happens to become zero at one sampling dimension is not reclassified as an ordinary constant there; the continuation is defined from the generic neighborhood. Degenerate sampling points must be excluded or treated by a separate resonant continuation, as the current generic-indicial check already requires.

This justifies discarding symbolically D-dependent columns for this physical target without proving each such column individually positive. It does not justify discarding an arbitrary solution's modes, taking a limit of individual masters before their rational weights, or erasing a nonzero logarithmic/negative-power remainder. The rational weights cannot turn a nonconstant D exponent into a constant branch, but they can lower a constant exponent and require extra series coefficients.

## Existing consumer and exact remaining checks

`src/engine/connection.rs` retains symbolic exponents through the existing preparation, verifies generic indicial sampling, constructs and matches the actual endpoint solution, then calls `project_limit` once per complete reduced target with all rational weights. `src/frobenius.rs::project_limit` skips columns whose exponent has nonzero epsilon derivative, expands the retained rational weights, checks sufficient series depth, combines coefficients by total power/log degree, and rejects an uncancelled divergence. Those are the correct operations for this bridge.

The partial-flow consumer must bind this call to the exact endpoint capability, family and original target identity. It must preserve the original uncancelled coefficient domain and the exact native target reconstruction. It must audit every relevant generated label and retain the actual complete regular-singular preparation rather than substituting sampled or guessed exponents. The existing sufficient-series and divergence failures must remain failures. Right-half-plane eta, the complete common-regulator order and the thermal uniformity hypothesis remain mandatory.

The theorem does not add a missing analytic period formula. Its remaining live requirement is a checked connection between these specific proof and transport objects. Focused regressions should include two columns whose exponents differ by an integer and cancel only after a pole weight; a constant-exponent/log cancellation across components; a true surviving divergence; insufficient series for an eta-pole reconstruction; and a varying exponent with an isolated integer collision. No tolerance or per-master shortcut is warranted.
