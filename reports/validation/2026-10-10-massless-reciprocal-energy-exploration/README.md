# Reciprocal occupied energies and polynomial source shifts

This exploration does not justify changing production massless admission.
Widening only the exact occupied-energy completion leaves all 19 polynomial
reduction probes unchanged. The two shorter reductions obtained after adding
normal-vector sources also follow from the same source rows restricted to their
original polynomial domains. No reciprocal factor is needed for those results.

The [assessment](assessment.md) gives a narrow joint dimensional-continuation
construction for finite inverse powers of a certified occupied energy. It
retains upper Fermi surfaces, strengthens the massless-origin bounds, and
distinguishes on-shell auxiliary weights from introducing a new uncut energy
pole. An independent review and 216 exact rational normal-source checks are
saved alongside it. This mathematical exploration does not admit those weights
into production source, boundary or cache contracts. The
[control plan](minimal-control-plan.md) distinguishes an elementary source/seed
check from a genuine two-loop flow validation.

The [native small controls](native-source-probe/README.md) regenerate the original
three-loop singleton geometry, verify that only completion 6 is exactly the
occupied energy, and compare five fresh-source presentations. Each native
program is encoded, decoded and replayed. Original polynomial targets, surface
terms and coefficient conditions are retained. The useful source shift reduces
the total number of right-hand-side terms from 192 to 179 across the 19 points;
domain widening alone leaves it at 192.

The subsequent [polynomial-only pilot](native-shifted-polynomial-pilot/README.md)
still fails to close. Its unresolved frontiers grow as 44, 152, 528, 1025, hitting
the same configured limit as the matched original-source baseline. Its final
original output rows contain 19 and 1061 terms, versus 19 and 1506 in the
baseline. The observed runtime was 163.911 seconds with 79,932 KiB peak child RSS;
shared-host timings are not a controlled performance comparison. A smaller
frontier does not establish a closed basis, boundary or numerical amplitude.

Production continues to use fixed cut shells, polynomial massless targets and
the existing native ordering. Three-loop numerical verification remains pending;
four-loop numerical scale-up remains gated on it. This evidence supports further
investigation of exact source presentation, rather than adding reciprocal-energy
admission on the basis of the observed reductions.

retains original and compressed hashes and sizes.
