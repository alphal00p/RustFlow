# Read-only assessment: reciprocal compact energies in the sealed massless class

Status: proposed narrow proof extension; no source, flow or boundary admission change. Original physical targets stay polynomial. This does not enlarge the structural endpoint classes or admit the unmerged prism parametric bridge.

## Admissible object

Only an exact completion `f_s=c_s E_i`, with nonzero rational `c_s`, for a future occupied loop covered by the existing sealed endpoint/origin evidence is in scope. All other positive completion powers remain invalid: virtual energies, energy sums, mixed Gram factors and arbitrary rational functions. The compact energy is eta-independent, so its reciprocal does not add an eta derivative or an ordinary virtual pole.

For each finite label define `p_i=sum_s max(n_s,0)` over certified completions of compact loop i. Ignoring any cancellations or positive-energy numerator improvements is conservative. Retain all original coefficient conditions and check roles before any zero classification. Each finite source identity/application gets a common high-D domain; no single fixed dimension is asserted for the full unbounded integer index box.

## Local origin distribution

At fixed positive eta, the current sealed proof supplies a UV-meromorphically continued smooth virtual/transfer test kernel near each compact origin. Multiplying that complete local kernel by `E_i^(-p_i)` changes only its finite radial valuation. It must not be described as a smooth multiplier at E=0.

For `d=D-1`, radial monomial `|q|^(2j)`, original energy monomial `E^r`, and cut C_n, integrating the spatial radius first gives

```
B_n(E) = A_d/2 (-1)^(n-1) binom(d/2+j-1,n-1) E^(d+2j+r-2n)
```

on E>0. Thus signed r, including r=-p, has the same meromorphic family. The compact bulk integral is the complete binomial divided by `beta=d+2j+r+1-2n`, times `mu^beta`; cancellations in binomial/beta occur exactly before assigning D. A surviving beta pole remains a genuine failure. The existing isolated continued moment API already implements this signed-r family; it does not itself authorize graph/source products.

For lower occupation contact H_ell(E), a sufficient finite-label bound is

```
Re D > 2*n + ell + p + 2*max(H_upper-1,0).
```

It ensures the required finite energy jets of the jointly integrated radial density vanish at zero. Define the joint product in this open high-D domain, then continue it meromorphically: the lower contact is zero in that specified family, not because support is empty or because E^-p is pointwise defined at zero. Positive upper contacts have separated support E=mu>0 and cause no new origin pole. The upper and lower distributions are never evaluated as an independent singular product. Positive cut indices and nonnegative occupations remain required.

One can establish total-derivative identities on E>rho with a smooth lower cutoff, after ordinary virtual UV continuation. For any fixed finite rational source, select D large enough that every source term and the finitely differentiated cutoff flux vanish as rho->0. This gives the same product and proves IBP before continuing D. A coordinatewise bound suffices at simultaneous origins because the only new denominators are products of individual energies; existing transfer/collinear bounds remain in force.

The reciprocal energy is external to virtual Gaussian/Schwinger integration, so it does not change UV parameter exponents, active virtual rank, or eta Mellin exponents. Retain the existing complete-cut prescription: independent same-sign line-mass Feynman regulators, removed at fixed positive T and eta inside the Fermi pole strip; then real-Fermi T->0; then the certified eta endpoint and meromorphic dimensional continuation. Consequently `|E_epsilon|^-p<=r^-p`; finite mass/energy derivatives add finite inverse powers and are dominated by the strengthened high-D bounds. ## Exact normal-source check

For raw native spatial shell moments (before external measure conversion), write M[n,p] for C_n E^-p H_upper,0 H_lower,0 and S[n,p] for C_n E^-p H_upper,1 H_lower,0. The total derivative with v=U/(2E) gives

```
0 = -n M[n+1,p] -(p+1)/2 M[n,p+2] -1/2 S[n,p+1]
    +1/2 (lower contact with C_n E^(-p-1)).
```

The lower term is zero only with the joint origin prescription. Dividing by the common angular/chemical factor, the identity reduces exactly to

```
-n binom(a,n) / beta
  + (p+1)/2 binom(a,n-1) / beta
  + 1/2 binom(a,n-1) = 0,
a=d/2-1, beta=d-p-1-2n.
```

This follows from n*binom(a,n)=(a-n+1)*binom(a,n-1), so the upper surface must be retained. It is a useful source replay and boundary regression, not a replacement physical answer.

1. Add a purely algebraic exact-energy factor descriptor, separate from the existing `mass_squared>0` nonsingularity certificate. A sealed massless permit wraps this descriptor with the new origin theorem/version and source options. Leave the old default constructor/behavior intact.
2. Audit p_i for every actual target, candidate and basis label; source proofs with symbolic unbounded indices are pointwise meromorphic identities, backed by each finite shifted row's high-D construction. Checked arithmetic and explicit label/rank limits remain mandatory.
3. Widen only those certified completion axes in source/admitted/zero domains. Bind the origin theorem, energy slots/coefficient/loop, exact family, source option and prescription into identity. Lower-contact and free-virtual zeros must share the widened admitted box and unchanged tail/occupation guards.
4. The existing normal-source generator is algebraically just a shifted U source. Its current documented precondition says E is nonzero on complete support; massless use needs the additional proved joint-continuation precondition instead of falsely claiming nonsingularity. No new elimination engine is required.
5. Boundary extraction must accept signed energy monomials only through that permit, use the existing signed continued massless bulk seed and finite upper-surface formula, and bind the changed origin proof in cache identity. Strip/restore these exact fixed-soft factors around ordinary region expansion as already done for massive energies; never pass them as ordinary virtual denominators.
6. Regressions should cover exact rational normal IBP including its nonzero upper term; lower-contact zero at finite p; a true dimensional pole and a removable ratio; fixed-label endpoint bounds increased by p; rejection of mixed/virtual energy factors, wrong family/options and mu=0; native guarded replay of original polynomial targets through inverse-energy intermediates; and shared boundary/AMF refinement. No discovery/closure improvement is established by this proof.

## Real remaining risks

The mathematical extension is narrow and plausible with the cutoff/regulated-shell argument above, but merely removing the two admission guards would violate the current contracts in geometry, boundary extraction, cache identity and source-normal documentation. Pointwise fixed-D products at the apex are not supplied by this proposal. The existing angular d>0 and cut-order numerical limits remain. A singular auxiliary basis moment at a sampled D must still fail or require an explicitly combined analytic evaluation, even if the physical target might cancel its pole. This extension does not resolve common-pole E8 contours or the separate unmerged parametric endpoint proof.
