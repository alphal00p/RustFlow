# Independent mathematical critique

The finite-data obstruction is unconditional. Given any fitted analytic germ F(t) and any observed prefix through t^(N-1), F(t)+t^N H(t) has the same prefix for arbitrary analytic H. Neither rank-one nullspace nor exact heldout zeros removes that freedom. Heldout data are an error detector, not a proof. The candidate must be selected using training alone and its JSON/digest frozen before reading the heldout file. A heldout failure ends that dataset's fit attempt; increasing the order against the same holdout would contaminate it.

There is a separate all-orders obstruction when the producer has only an asymptotic expansion. A term such as exp(-eta) is invisible to every inverse-power coefficient on the positive ray. Thus an all-n recurrence for region coefficients does not, by itself, certify the physical integral unless convergence or an appropriate uniqueness class is proved. An integrand-level telescoping identity with valid boundary treatment is stronger: it applies to the integral before expansion and can then be continued under the declared regulator prescription.

Fixed rational D can conceal rank changes, exponent collisions, poles and disappearing channels. Even an exact certified equation at D=13/2 is a fixed-D result. It cannot certify the epsilon Laurent expansion or meromorphic-D family without a symbolic-D proof. Nonzero leading coefficients and startup data must be tracked explicitly; recurrence singularities at integer n prevent naive forward propagation.

The best initial certificate target is a scalar fixed-domain parameter representation with rational shift quotients/logarithmic derivatives. The finite ansatz in design.md is a conservative search, not a completeness theorem. Multiple summation partitions, unsupported boundary faces or a certificate needing new poles may make that search fail even when the integral is holonomic. That outcome should guide the next mathematical representation, not justify larger fitting budgets or a claim that no ODE exists.

Boundary certificates are essential: Koutschan's primary account constructs a telescoper together with a certificate and obtains the integrated equation from the associated boundary contribution; examples include inhomogeneous recurrences when a boundary does not vanish. The compact and Fermi faces in this task must therefore be kept until their contributions are proved or explicitly represented. See [Holonomic Anti-Differentiation and Feynman Amplitudes, section 2 and section 3.2](https://www.ricam.oeaw.ac.at/files/reports/20/rep20-44.pdf). No evaluated examples from that paper are used as data or reference answers.

A bounded eta branch cut would not by itself certify continuation to eta=0. Compact external momenta alone do not establish such a cut bound: a parameter-domain proof must also control degenerating virtual/UV faces. Even if an exact scalar representation proves a finite spectral interval, the physical endpoint may be an endpoint of that cut. A conformal map then normally places the requested endpoint on the unit circle, where a 64-term geometric remainder loses its contraction. A tail estimate still needs a proved analytic norm, endpoint exponent and branch prescription. Raised distributions and signed numerator channels do not supply a positive Stieltjes measure automatically. Thus no general endpoint error bound follows from compact support plus a finite coefficient prefix. This is a mathematical limitation of the proposed fallback, not evidence of divergence for the current integral.
