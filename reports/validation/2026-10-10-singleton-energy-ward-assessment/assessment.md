The normalized-global-boost C1 Ward identity extends to finite polynomial powers of exact pure occupied-energy completions. No reciprocal-energy source domain is required. This is a derivation and exact moment assessment, not a production change or evidence of closure improvement.

Retain the existing sealed SingletonGerm/common-origin assumptions, positive chemical magnitude mu, selected shell q0²=0 at C1, and lower occupation index zero. Every ordinary completion depending on the medium must either be exactly c E0 with a certified nonzero rational c and nonpositive base index, or have its base index fixed to zero. All mixed and virtual energies therefore remain absent. Gram-dependent completions and physical factors retain the original polynomial/domain bounds. Matching only on a shell, on a target numerator name, or at one numerical point is insufficient.

Let k=−sum_j a_j over the exact pure-energy slots. It is a nonnegative integer for every admitted finite label. The simultaneous vector field

`v_i = (E_i q0 − (q_i.q0) U) / E0`

annihilates every Lorentz Gram scalar. Its complete divergence is `D−2+q0²/E0²`; its action on E0 is `E0−q0²/E0`. Therefore the product of pure-energy completion powers contributes k modulo terms proportional to q0². At C1 those terms vanish after the same regulated high-D origin construction used for the existing raw Ward identity. No forbidden source seed at a positive energy-completion index is imported. The proof uses an origin cutoff before its removal, not a pointwise division at E0=0.

The remaining polynomial identities are

`(D−2+k) I_0 − I_1[E0] = 0`,

`(D−2+k) I_s + s I_(s+1)[E0] = 0`, for s≥1.

The constants c_j contribute the same fixed monomial normalization to both terms and do not alter k. Equivalently the diagonal is `D−2−sum_j a_j`. The source's energy insertion has degree one and is converted through the exact assigned factor map. Arbitrary finite polynomial k improves the own-origin power relative to k=0. Virtual UV continuation, fixed-positive-eta/fixed-T regulator removal, other existing proof conditions, and the subsequent dimensional continuation retain their old order. An unbounded integer source axis is interpreted pointwise through a common high-D domain for each finite row/application, not one dimension uniform over all integers.

Own Cn with n>1 remains excluded in this nonfactorized theorem. The terms q0² Cn become C_(n−1), and the inverse-energy contacts no longer vanish. A simple diagnostic is the Lorentz-scalar factor F(q0²)=1+q0² at C2. The C2 contribution then contains both M2 and M1; assigning a single D−4+k coefficient leaves the nonzero residual −2 M1. This distinguishes the proposed C1 theorem from a truly factorized raised shell, where the rest has no q0 dependence and the existing local dilation has coefficient D−2n.

It also checks nonzero counterexamples to the unchanged old diagonal at k>0 and to the C2 extension with an off-cone Gram factor. No native predictions or oracle values are used.
