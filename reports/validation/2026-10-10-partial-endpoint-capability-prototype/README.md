# Isolated rank-two partial endpoint evidence

The prototype constructs a sealed `PartialEndpointEvidence` from the copied, frozen partial-origin capability and replays its complete actual native HEPKit support, off-null channel and chart data. It then computes exact finite-label endpoint inequalities. It provides no values or physical-flow admission.

The final standalone gate passes 18 tests: eight new endpoint tests and all ten inherited origin tests. `final-gates.json` binds the binary, build, runner, log and separately named origin/endpoint evidence. The source is under `source/`; `source-and-proof-binding.json` binds each source, the independent mathematics reviews and the compile-time fixture. `build-00` preserves the initial Rational-reference compilation failure; `build-01` is successful. No Cargo or production source was changed. Earlier diagnostic runs remain distinct. The `historical-shared-output` run passed but its shared output directory allowed identically named origin reports to overwrite endpoint reports; it is not the final endpoint evidence.

## Implemented contract

- Construction requires exact input, family, deformation and source-option identity, then reconstructs and compares every origin support/chart/channel record. Missing or modified evidence fails, including when initial labels vanish.
- Origin and endpoint identities remain distinct. All fields carrying authority are private and there is no deserialization constructor.
- Every label is role-validated before a zero classification. Positive completion powers, negative occupations and nonzero storage tails reject. Family binding also applies to an empty label request.
- Actual positive virtual degree P, positive pure-transfer degree P0, polynomial momentum degree R and compact cut/upper/lower jet orders J_i are retained. Each finite label receives exact rational D, Taylor count N and sigma=1/2, with strictly positive virtual, UV, angular, radial and lower-contact margins. N means subtraction of degrees 0 through N-1.
- All three actual placements are exercised: singleton cut[3]/shift[0] and double cut[0,3]/shift[1,2] or shift[2,4]. Twenty-seven combinations of raised cut, upper and lower jets also retain explicit positive margins.
- Shifted-positive supports with V=0 remain nonzero at finite eta. Their endpoint classification is a separate report field and cannot supply native source-zero rules.
- Negative gates cover wrong binding/options, incomplete charts/off-null records, arithmetic and budget failures, invalid roles and nonpositive/tampered witnesses.

## Mathematical scope and required consumers

The bound is the reviewed h<=2 finite-J+N Taylor/Hölder theorem, with exact full-channel independent line-mass regulator identity, right-half-plane eta and the stated fixed-positive-T regulator removal followed by the real thermal limit and meromorphic D continuation. The additive thermal review supplies uniform small-real-T occupation-pairing control; it does not permit arbitrary joint complex regulator/T limits. The independent source and thermal reviews are hash-bound in the proof binding.

The constructor audits original target labels. A production consumer must separately audit every actual generated basis, candidate and source-application label; preserve raw coefficient poles before cancellation or zero classification and check them at every epsilon sample; and audit the complete rational target reconstruction, including all eta poles. The audit explicitly reports these coefficient and combined-target checks as incomplete.

An actual regular-singular Frobenius representation, sufficient series order, combined-target projector and uncancelled-divergence check remain separate requirements. Boundary/infinity integration and its region ownership are also separate. This type supplies no native mode, boundary, raw Ward, finite-eta source-zero or production admission authority.

Adapter budgets cover labels, axes, finite degrees, witness arithmetic and the inherited proof budgets. They do not bound internal CAS scratch/operation complexity, and this prototype does not accept RunContext cancellation. Those remain implementation gates, not implied guarantees. No numerical period or transport comparison was attempted.

## Reproduction

`source/build.py` records the standalone rustc command and exact library paths used. Recreate the recorded /tmp source layout from `source/` and the unchanged copied origin libraries before invoking it. `run.py` checks the saved executable hash and runs the 18-test filter with separate evidence directories. All successful build and execution bytes are recorded; recompilation is not needed to verify the saved artifacts. Do not run the runner over this frozen report: use a fresh output location for any new execution.
