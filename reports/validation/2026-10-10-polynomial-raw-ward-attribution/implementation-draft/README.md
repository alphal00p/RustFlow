# Narrow polynomial raw Ward implementation draft

This patch is **not applied to production, typechecked, or executed**. Rustfmt
parsed its three staged Rust files. It is an additive source-owner proposal;
it does not enable a physical-flow dispatch, install analytic values, or claim
native closure. Promotion depends on the current complete polynomial closure
pilot and subsequent proof/replay/numerical gates.

`source-map.json` binds the exact production bases and staged outputs;
`raw-ward.patch` is the reviewable diff. The complete staged files are preserved
under `source/`. The independent proof and scope are in the parent directory's
`raw-polynomial-Ward-owner-plan.md` and `raw-Ward-review.json`. That earlier
review binds the report-only probe snapshot, not this uncompiled draft.

## Additive API and owner boundaries

* `MasslessFlowEvidence::singleton_raw_ward_evidence` is private to the crate
  and matches the actual sealed `SingletonGerm` enum. Its unforgeable payload
  binds the actual coordinates, deformed factors, roles, occupied shell and
  original proof identity. It verifies the exact null quadratic, positive mu,
  two endpoint factors, physical scalar-factor invariance and every ordinary
  factor's dependence on all medium coordinates. No topology, source name or
  fixture slot controls admission.
* `WeightedMeasure::raw_singleton_ward_sources` is private to the crate. It
  rechecks that payload against the actual measure and converts the actual
  occupied energy through the complete inverse-propagator basis. Source bases
  have C1, lower H0 and zero indices for every medium-dependent factor; Gram
  completions keep nonpositive bounds and virtual physical powers remain
  unrestricted. Backend tails remain fixed zero. It emits the two raw upper
  branches, with no reciprocal factors or new zero domains. Two domain cases
  and an explicit affine monomial bound are checked before returning rows.
* `OccupiedCutFamily::guarded_sources_with_massless_raw_ward` is the sole new
  public factory. It prefixes these two rows to the caller's existing source
  presentation and adds the theorem/representation identity to the full native
  context. Other sealed classes fail this explicit factory. Existing factories,
  `WeightedSourceOptions`, serialization, defaults, source-domain bounds,
  boundary owners and flow/assembly dispatch remain unchanged.

The choice of the raw, unrecentered pair follows the matched local controls:
upper recentering is mathematically equivalent but changes search chronology.
This draft does not add the four shifted polynomial boost rows or four shifted
U rows. Those are separate presentation experiments and must retain their own
complete source/condition/domain pullbacks if added later.

## Focused tests included in the patch

1. Actual assigned sunset geometries with ordinary, nonunit rescaled and sheared
   routing. Reconstruct the emitted energy coefficient from every actual factor
   monomial; test C1, upper branches, all energy-base guards, lower guard,
   physical virtual powers, tails, exact eta binding, symbol collisions and
   an insufficient domain budget.
2. Typed singleton admission and full factory identity: reject multicuts,
   wrong family/deformation/options, positive mass, zero chemical endpoint,
   and inverse-energy options; preserve the existing lower-origin zero and
   polynomial domain. Old and augmented native contexts reject cross-decoding.
3. Native source-only discovery of the bulk Ward rule, exact encode/decode and
   replay, with actual factor lookup; raised cuts and an extra energy power
   remain uncovered. This is a bounded native algebra test, not a flow result.
4. Independent exact rational radial actions of H0 and H1 through H5 at several
   dimensions and nonunit chemical potentials. Upper surfaces are nonzero and
   the bulk/positive-H source signs cancel exactly.

All these tests are pending execution. Before promotion, the native test's
current default discovery budget and the nonunit input fixture must be checked
on the coordinated build. A generated-rule domain never grants admission to
all source bases in its polynomial image: for example the E numerator term is
a shift of a source with energy index zero, not a new inverse-energy domain.

The strongest full pilot must retain every original target, coefficient
condition and derivative audit. Shorter local right-hand sides alone cannot
justify promotion or a claim that the three-loop feature is complete.
