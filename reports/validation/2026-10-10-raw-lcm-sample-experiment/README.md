The raw-LCM sample prototype preserves exact results but does not justify promotion. All five focused tests and all 72 sample comparisons pass. Every one of the 24 raw trials was admitted under the declared limits, so the measured result does not conceal a fallback. The sum of per-sample median times was 6.222 seconds for native sequential addition, 6.078 seconds for balanced rational addition, and 5.862 seconds for raw LCM: only 3.6% below balanced. Individual samples range from 23.9% faster to 36.3% slower. No full target run or production change is recommended.

Only the eight previously captured costly repeated-denominator buffers were used. The same frozen native coefficient table was restored from its gzip archive with exact raw/compressed hashes, normalized on import, and then admitted through a private CanonicalBatch check. Canonical input normalization is performed once outside the timed methods and recorded separately. Three rotated repetitions compare sequential, balanced and raw-LCM methods against the same exact captured sum. This selected sample is not representative of all pending-label additions.

The prototype first forms a polynomial denominator LCM with native GCDs and exact quotients, forms the polynomial numerator sum over that denominator, and performs one final native canonical normalization. Temporary numerator/denominator pairs are never represented as Coefficients. Zero numerator is recognized exactly over a nonzero formal polynomial denominator. Inputs with zero denominators, noncanonical fractions/signs, mismatched variable maps or more than 32 entries are rejected.

Explicit degree, sparse/dense-support, coefficient-bit, operand bit-product and cumulative operation estimates precede arithmetic. A preflight decline discards scratch and returns to the unchanged original sequential order on the intact canonical inputs. Native arithmetic failure, unexpected nonexact quotient or output-cap failure remains fatal and cannot trigger that fallback. The limits bound declared input/work estimates and retained outputs; they do not constitute a complete bound on internal Symbolica GCD scratch. Every cap and observed maximum is recorded in summary.json/results.json.

Tests cover generic symbolic rational/sign/cancellation cases, canonical-boundary rejection, each preflight-limit fallback with exact original-order equality, fatal nonexact division, and a 32-input cancellation/growth control. The first test build had one borrow-checking error; its exact source/log are retained in history/. No mathematical behavior was changed to resolve it.

The source-only experiment uses pinned libraries and never builds or edits shared production targets. All source/executable/CAS/sample bindings and per-operation timing records are retained. There are no active readers or writers. The existing generic-epsilon endpoint and native proof semantics are unchanged.
