This is a proposed generic finite-positive-eta source/origin capability for at most two unrestricted virtual loops. It is a mathematical and algorithm audit, not production code, a completed native certificate implementation, an endpoint theorem, or a numerical result. It does not change the existing all-uncut admission. The separately frozen small-family proofs provide examples, not the authority for this generalization.

The proposal is viable with explicit finite-jet hypotheses. It does not need a kernel holomorphic to all orders at one fixed D. It does need a universal statement of the form: for every finite admitted integer label and every finite required test-function jet, a computable open high-D neighborhood gives the needed regulated jets. Checking a finite initial target list alone cannot authorize an unbounded native lower-contact zero box. Either seal this universal finite-jet schema and instantiate its bounds for consumed labels, or restrict the native zero domains to a finite certified index box and change the source identity when enlarging it.

## Exact structural assumptions

Retain the complete actual physical inventory, independently of the shifted subset. In occupied coordinates every virtual-dependent physical row must be an exact rational momentum square `(a_e k + c_e q)^2`, with zero physical mass. There are h<=2 unrestricted virtual coordinates and finitely many occupied future null vectors q_i. Physical denominators cannot depend on the medium vector or another fixed external scale. Completions and inactive physical factors are polynomial insertions only; no reciprocal compact energy, Gram denominator, or rational compact numerator is admitted. Validate all cut/occupation roles, positive chemical magnitudes, physical/input/storage arities, and the full family/target binding first.

For the proposed placement solve exactly for one affine virtual translation `k=k'+Tq` making every unshifted physical row independent of all compact q. Its unit block-triangular Jacobian, transformed rows and original polynomial identities are part of the proof. Every nonzero purely compact physical row must be shifted. Auxiliary coefficients are positive rationals (initial implementation may deliberately require the existing unit coefficient); phases/normalizations of the original physical factors remain external and exact. A shifted pure compact row is not part of the virtual mass sum S.

For each actual positive virtual support, construct the Gaussian A=sum(alpha a a^T), B=sum(alpha a c^T), C=sum(alpha c c^T), U=det A, mean cofactors adj(A)B and V=U*C-B^T adj(A)B. These must match the actual HEPKit/full-kinematic polynomials after the recorded routing and parameter map. No null-only specialization may replace the full identity, and no shifted-only row list may replace the full support. The exact Schur inequality is `0<=Q=C-B^T A^-1 B<=C<=K*S*I`, where S sums only active shifted virtual parameters with their positive auxiliary coefficients. K is retained as an exact finite rational upper bound, for example a conservative sum of squared external row norms divided by the corresponding auxiliary coefficient.

That PSD inequality is not a Minkowski contour theorem. Separately require a replayed full-kinematic decomposition of the effective mass into `eta*S + sum Q_ij*h_ij + sum W_i*q_i^2 + M_reg`, with nonnegative physical future-difference channel coefficients and real W_i, or another already sealed common-contour certificate of equal strength. Under the actual independent same-sign line-mass regulators with real spatial momenta, require Re(F)>=eta*S. For one occupied vector the sole q^2 invariant is imaginary; for several vectors, the full channel proof is indispensable. Pure compact shifted denominators need their own uniform positive-eta gap. An unshifted pure transfer, or an unproved timelike sum channel, fails admission.

## Complete supports and rank-two charts

Enumerate every positive virtual support. Nonpositive indices give finite polynomial insertions before classification; there is no continuation through deleted Schwinger parameters.

* Rank below h: a retained exact rational null vector gives an unrestricted ordinary polynomial virtual direction. This is the existing separated ordinary zero proof, with all actual rows included. It does not declare the compact integral scaleless.
* Full rank and no shifted-positive virtual row: after the common translation every positive virtual denominator is a homogeneous massless vacuum row. A shifted compact-only transfer may still be present. The independent regulator-removal argument below supplies the ordinary vacuum zero; it is not zero at finite line regulators.
* Full rank with a shifted-positive virtual row: construct the chart family below. V=0 is not a finite-eta zero. Factorized scaleless subgraphs may cancel only after the complete analytically continued chart sum; no individual chart is silently removed.

For h=1 choose the largest alpha as projective gauge alpha_i=1. U has the positive constant a_i^2, so the closed chart has no D-dependent inverse-U coordinate. There are |support| charts.

For h=2 choose alpha_i largest, then alpha_j largest among rows nonparallel to a_i. Set alpha_i=1, alpha_j=x. For every other row transverse to a_i put alpha_e=x*y_e; for a parallel row put alpha_e=z_e. All x,y,z lie in [0,1]. The charts indexed by ordered independent pairs (i,j) cover the projective domain up to ordering ties of measure zero. Their Jacobian is x^(m-1), with m the number of transverse rows. This is a complete cover with at most |support|*(|support|-1) charts, not a bounded search for favorable charts.

Every nonzero Cauchy–Binet monomial of U contains a transverse parameter and therefore x. The i,j term supplies the nonzero constant det(a_i,a_j)^2 after division. Thus U=x*U0 with U0 a polynomial having nonnegative rational coefficients and a strict constant lower bound. At x=0 all remaining virtual rows are parallel; adj(A)B vanishes. Consequently every mean cofactor and every entry of V is divisible by x. Exact collected polynomial division must verify these identities. Q and the Gaussian means are smooth rational functions with denominator U0 on the closed cube. Covariance adj(A)/U has at most one additional inverse power of x per covariance factor. No inverse external Gram projection is used.

An explicit algebraic form makes this check reusable. After a constant rational virtual basis change putting the primary row along (1,0), write A=[[L+x*Abar,x*Bbar],[x*Bbar,x*Cbar]], with L>=1 and Cbar bounded below by the squared transverse pivot component. Then U=x*[L*Cbar+x*(Abar*Cbar-Bbar^2)]. Write each external column of B as (T+x*Tbar,x*Wbar). Both entries of adj(A)B have a factor x. These are finite polynomial identities, independently replayable against the native U/B/C data; they do not evaluate a period. Keep the basis determinant in the bound/provenance if using this normalization.

## Finite jets and regulated UV continuation

Let P be the sum of all positive virtual powers. Let R conservatively bound covariance inverse-x powers in the unprojected Gaussian polynomial numerator; the total weighted momentum degree of all inactive physical/completion factors is a safe finite upper bound. Quadratic factors count degree two and medium-energy factors degree one; exact factor maps determine the degree. Let J bound the finite derivatives of the virtual test kernel actually required by the distributions. It includes required-cut mass jets, upper-surface derivatives, and, crucially, lower-contact derivatives: H_lower=ell requires up to ell-1 test-function derivatives. An implementation may retain the conservative sum of these orders and verify the distribution normalization expansion; it must not borrow an all-orders germ to omit the lower jets. Fixed-spatial inverse shell Jacobians are accounted for separately in the compact radial inequality.

A Gaussian tensor term has effective-mass power lambda=h*D/2-P+c with c>=0. Physical jets lower this power by at most J. For h=2, before UV continuation the only D-growing projective singularity is `x^(P_trans-1-D/2-R)` times a function whose other parameter weights have nonnegative integer powers. A fixed Taylor subtraction order N>Re(D)/2+R is conservative. On a sufficiently small generic D neighborhood choose N<Re(D)/2+R+2. Taking Re(D)>2(P+R+J+2) gives Re(lambda)-J-N>0. J is counted once in this inequality. For h=1 there is no corresponding UV face and the same bound is more than sufficient.

At fixed positive eta and positive T, with every independent line regulator nonzero, the prescribed F is nonzero throughout a closed chart: if S>0 its real part is positive; if S=0 the affine criterion implies Q=0 and the remaining regulator mass average has one nonzero common Feynman phase. First give the active propagator powers independent complex analytic indices, with real parts large enough for the fixed finite polynomial numerator in the regulated Euclidean/Gaussian representation. This supplies a common joint dimension/index convergence domain; a D-only absolute-convergence strip is not assumed for arbitrary numerator degrees. The standard finite Taylor identities continue that same analytic family meromorphically to the required positive integer powers and the high-D neighborhood. Equality in the joint convergence domain fixes the prescription without an arbitrary subtraction or contact constant. Deleted factors are handled as explicit polynomial insertions in their own support, not silently reached by changing the support during this continuation. At high D, all derivatives needed up to J+N have nonnegative remaining F powers and are uniformly bounded as the line regulators vanish. Smooth U0, Q and mean cofactors ensure a finite bound polynomial in external compact energies. A bounded imaginary D neighborhood controls the power branch and logarithmic D derivatives. The counterterm denominators are meromorphic and avoided on the proof neighborhood. This establishes finitely many jets, not all-order smoothness at that D.

For S identically zero, also Q=0, so F is just the linear regulator mass average. Let delta=max epsilon_e. Its parameter derivatives are O(delta); the same finite-subtraction argument bounds every required mass/test jet by a positive power of delta, uniformly when regulator ratios approach zero. Hence the translated ordinary vacuum tends to zero in the common high-D domain, before meromorphic continuation. This proof applies to rank-two vacuum supports with arbitrary finite rational row inventories, using the same max/nonparallel chart cover. It must not be replaced by a bare homogeneity assertion.

For S>0, the argument only proves finite-eta continuity/jets. It does not establish eta->0, a uniform eta/temperature limit, or a large-eta boundary. All source identities are continued from the common finite-jet neighborhood. A fixed finite source application has finitely many shifted labels, so intersecting their high-D neighborhoods is legitimate. The theorem is pointwise over the integer lattice; one fixed D need not handle every possible integer index.

For a lower contact at compact loop i, retain an explicit radial margin, conservatively `Re(D)>2*n_i+ell_i+2*(s_i-1)_+` together with the corresponding finite-jet bounds for the other compact factors and a positive Gram-angle measure domain. Choose a common high-D neighborhood for all compact factors, not just the selected lower face. At fixed T the real Fermi tail dominates the finite polynomial energy bound. Remove same-sign line regulators within the pole-free Fermi strip before the real-Fermi T limit. No pointwise product E^-p delta^(ell)(E), arbitrary complex shell shift, or new reciprocal completion is defined by this proof.

## Proposed sealed algorithm and limits

The constructor takes the actual prepared family, cuts, shifted physical slots/coefficient map, source options and a proof budget. It validates roles/masses/arity/chemical assignments, binds all raw target/source coefficient conditions, reconstructs every physical row from the actual factor map, verifies the affine witness, checks all compact-only gaps/common channels, then enumerates the complete positive supports. Every nonzero support records native/full-kinematic polynomial identities and its complete chart list; every zero support records its separate null/vacuum reason. A consumed support cannot be inferred from a different support or an old all-mass token.

Budgets cover the entire operation, including failed attempts and replay: row count, supports, total charts, total retained polynomial monomials, rational arithmetic operations/coefficient bit size, numerator expansion terms, and cancellation checks. Preflight bounds include 2^n supports, at most n*2^(n-1) rank-one charts and n*(n-1)*2^(n-2) rank-two ordered-pair charts. Checked arithmetic must reject overflow before enumeration. No partial certificate is returned on exhaustion. Concrete numerical defaults need measured implementation gates; this design does not claim a cost improvement.

The sealed capability should own only finite-positive-eta source/origin authority. Illustrative interfaces are `validate_family_and_placement`, `certified_lower_origin_loops`, `certified_virtual_zero_domains`, `audit_finite_labels`, and a stable `source_identity`. It does not expose an endpoint projection or boundary builder. Existing singleton raw-Ward evidence is a separate theorem and must not be inferred from this new token merely because one cut is present. Endpoint and infinity evidence remain separately sealed. Preserve the old all-uncut constructor and identity unchanged.

Bind the complete physical inventory, exact affine/virtual routing and determinants, shifted and purecompact sets, positive auxiliary coefficients, native U/F identities, support/chart witnesses, universal finite-jet theorem version, per-label bound data, common regulator prescription, full admitted domains, source options and original conditions. Native source replay must see a different context identity. Final target/derivative audit remains mandatory; this theorem grants no closure or master promotion.

Required negatives include the shifted-only rank counterexample from the frozen admission design (a surviving one-mass vacuum is not a free direction), shifted-positive V=0, an unshifted compact transfer, a timelike channel, failed affine translation, a medium-dependent physical factor, physical mass, inverse completion, invalid H or storage tail, wrong family/options/shift map, tampered cofactor division, omitted support/chart and every exhausted budget. Required positives include equivalent double-mask affine routings, parallel/antiparallel rows, independent and coupled rank-two supports, arbitrarily chosen finite raised/lower/upper jets instantiated by explicit bounds, and identical native source/replay semantics under rational routing changes. Tests of original conditions must include poles in cut-vanishing and algebraically canceled target terms.

The mathematical finite-jet route has no remaining graph-specific assumption in the rank<=2 chart lemma, but it still needs an independently reviewed implementation that replays actual native/full-kinematic identities and the common contour certificate. Until those exist, this is a viable design, not a sealed production permit. In particular, the already passing small-family charts and closure result cannot substitute for the generic checks above.

## Bounded exact algebra replay

`chart_check.py` checks all 144 supports of five exact rational row inventories, including sheared, nonunit, parallel and antiparallel rows. Its 115 full-rank supports give 656 complete max/nonparallel charts. Every rank-two chart replays both the two-row Cauchy–Binet identity for U and the three-row polarized bordered-Gram identities for every external V entry, then checks the positive unit bound and exact mean/V divisibility by x. There are also 80 finite J+N witnesses and the explicit shifted-only-rank negative. The run used 244,836 counted rational polynomial operations under a 2,000,000 limit. Run `python3 chart_check.py` from any directory to reproduce `chart-checks.json`.

This test independently exercises the algebra used in the proof; its JSON deliberately records that it is not native HEPKit replay or a physical-contour certificate. A future sealed owner must reconstruct/replay the actual input's full-kinematic polynomials and complete actual support cover, rather than recognize any of these test row inventories.
