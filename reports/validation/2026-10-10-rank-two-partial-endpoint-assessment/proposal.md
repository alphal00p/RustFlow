# Rank-at-most-two partial-placement endpoint assessment

This is a proof proposal, separate from the frozen finite-positive-eta origin prototype. No production endpoint permit, flow, integration shortcut, or numerical comparison is introduced. The proposed class retains the exact structural/channel hypotheses of that prototype, including unit eta masses and shifted purecompact factors c(qi-qj). The argument below concerns every finite label; it is not authority from an initial target list.

## Why the finite-jet estimate may extend

On each retained rank-two max/nonparallel chart, U=x*U0 with U0 bounded away from zero. Mean coefficients and Q=V/U are smooth rational functions on the closed cube, and covariance contributes at most a finite x^-R. For an unprojected Gaussian tensor term, the effective-mass exponent is lambda=h*D/2-P+c, c>=0. Parameter Taylor subtraction uses N terms (degrees 0,...,N-1), while the physical cut/occupation jets spend at most J derivatives. There are no inverse external Gram factors.

Choose a small generic D neighborhood with, for h=2,

    N > Re(D)/2 + R,
    N < Re(D)/2 + R + 2,
    Re(lambda) - J - N > sigma,     0 < sigma < 1.

For h=1 take N=0. A convenient stronger common bound is Re(D)>2(P+R+J+3) with sigma=1/2. Fix N on that neighborhood; avoid Gamma and Taylor-counterterm poles. The analytic family is fixed by the common joint analytic-index/Gaussian convergence domain before continuation, exactly as in the origin proof. No D-only bare UV convergence strip is assumed.

The full off-null channel identity and actual same-sign line-mass poles give

    Re F(eta,epsilon) >= eta*S + sum Q_ij*h_ij >= 0,
    h_ij = 2*r_i*r_j*(1-cos(theta_ij)).

Here F denotes the effective mass after division by U; all Q_ij are smooth on the chart. This inequality is valid for the actual future square-root poles with real spatial momenta, not arbitrary complex-scaled null vectors. The independent shell diagonals contribute purely imaginary terms. S is only the shifted-positive virtual mass sum; shifted purecompact transfers are handled below.

At eta=epsilon=0, F0=sum Q_ij*h_ij can vanish on parameter faces, origins, or collinear angular strata. Positivity alone would not justify the limit. The strict **remaining positive power** above is what controls it: every required derivative up to J+N of F^lambda is a finite sum with exponent lambda-j, j<=J+N, times bounded parameter coefficients and a finite polynomial in compact energies. Complex powers on the prescribed closed right-half-plane extend continuously at F=0, and with the strict sigma margin their differences obey a sigma-Holder estimate. Thus virtual IR/collinear zeros do not create a new inverse-F singularity in this high-D domain.

For rho=eta+max(sqrt(epsilon_e)), on bounded compact energies all F/mean derivative differences are O(rho), except the explicit shell inverse-energy factors treated separately. On finite-T tails the bound is O(rho) times a finite energy polynomial. The inequality |z^beta-w^beta|<=C|z-w|^sigma times a polynomial bound applies uniformly for beta in the chosen D neighborhood with Re(beta)>sigma and z,w in the prescribed closed sector. Bounded arguments also control finite D derivatives with a slightly smaller margin. Applying it before the x Taylor remainder estimate gives an integrable x power and a uniform O(rho^sigma) virtual difference. Taylor counterterms inherit the same estimate; their meromorphic denominators stay away from zero in the proof neighborhood.

If a full-rank support has Q=0 but S>0, this proves a vanishing physical endpoint as eta/regulators vanish, although the support is nonzero at finite eta. If S=0, the independently regulated vacuum factor vanishes as in the origin proof. Rank-deficient polynomial virtual directions remain their separate ordinary-zero case. None may be mislabeled a finite-eta zero merely from Q=0.

## Compact factors and mass jets

At fixed eta>0 the origin proof could use the compact gap. At eta=0 this gap disappears. Let P0 be the total positive power of purecompact physical transfers, and let J_i bound the mass/upper/lower jets assigned to compact loop i. A conservative common J may also be used for every loop. Leibniz differentiation raises the total transfer power by at most J. The shell inverse-energy factors cost at most r_i^-2J_i, as in the existing origin proof. All inactive factors/completions are finite polynomials and cause no new inverse compact factors.

For an actual shifted transfer, the off-null inequality gives

    |eta + c^2*h_ij + regulator terms|^-p <= const*h_ij^-p.

A difference estimate costs at most one additional sigma in transfer powers: |A^-p-B^-p|<=C|A-B|^sigma*B^(-p-sigma) for Re(A)>=B>0. With Q=P0+J+sigma, generalized Holder on the full normalized product angular measure bounds every differentiated transfer product by pair moments of t_ij^-Q. Pair independence is not required. The sufficient angular condition is

    Re(D)/2 - 1 > Q.

For P0=0 no Holder division by zero is used. Re(D)>k+2 ensures a positive real-dimensional Gram/angular measure for the k occupied vectors; prove the estimate for real D in an open interval and continue the resulting analytic family, rather than applying Holder to a complex measure.

A conservative radial integrability bound is

    Re(D) - 2 - 2*J_i - Q - sigma > 0.

The extra sigma accommodates the difference of inverse shell factors. For E_epsilon=sqrt(r^2-i*epsilon), |E_epsilon|>=r and |E_epsilon-r|<=C*sqrt(epsilon); interpolation gives a difference estimate with one extra r^-sigma. Larger finite polynomial tail powers remain harmless at fixed T.

Lower contacts also need vanishing jets, not just radial integrability. Strengthen the origin inequality to

    Re(D) > 2*n_i + ell_i + 2*(s_i-1)_+ + Q + sigma.

These conservative inequalities have a common sufficiently high-D domain for every finite label and every finite set of source/target/basis labels. They retain all raised-cut contacts and do not replace q_i^2*C_n by zero when n>1. J is spent once in the virtual exponent; bounding all Leibniz allocations by the same total J in the separate transfer/radial bounds is a conservative maximum, not an extra derivative of the virtual factor.

Physical mass jets are taken at fixed independent line regulators, with the original polynomial numerator held fixed. Their explicit derivative formulas are bounded at the zero physical masses using |E_epsilon|>=r. The proof does not assume a uniform two-sided real/complex mass neighborhood: negative real masses at arbitrarily small r would invalidate that assumption. One may equivalently take the one-sided physical mass jets when their equivalence has been retained from the original distribution construction. The limit of the already differentiated regulated kernels is what the uniform estimates control.

## Thermal order and the actual endpoint consumer

Remove independent line regulators at fixed T>0 inside the pole-free Fermi strip, while eta may tend to zero under the same estimates. Real Fermi factors have exponential tails that dominate the finite energy polynomial. For upper derivatives use the original normalized distribution identity/integration by parts against the uniformly controlled compact kernel jets; do not try to dominate a T^-s occupation derivative pointwise. Lower endpoint terms vanish by the strengthened radial jet condition. Then take the real-Fermi T->0 limit and meromorphically continue D. This preserves the existing thermal prescription; no unrestricted joint complex-regulator/T limit is asserted.

The proposal supplies a physical high-D limit plus a positive Holder remainder. It does not by itself instantiate an endpoint-mode owner. To bind it to the shared AMF projector, audit every consumed label and the original weighted target, preserve raw coefficient conditions, and retain the actual regular-singular Frobenius construction. On a generic high-D neighborhood the convergent target has no uncanceled negative-power or constant-log term. Dimension-dependent Frobenius powers that survive must vanish in that neighborhood; their dimensional continuation is discarded by the existing projector. Dimension-independent regular constants survive, and singular rational reconstruction weights must be combined before projection, as the current owner does. A formal positive F alone supplies none of these mode/reconstruction checks. The proof consumer must explicitly connect the uniform estimate to that shared regular-singular representation and its exact exponent/condition data.

## Distinct physical singleton zero

The current `MasslessFlowEvidence::new` plus its private `CertifiedEndpointClass::SingletonGerm` and `validate_labels` already bind an all-uncut homotopy, common origin prescription and finite cut/upper jets for one occupied null vector. A future narrowly sealed physical-singleton-zero evaluator may use that **endpoint theorem** without claiming finite-eta closure or transport, preserving all raw target poles and exact original distribution terms. Its typed check must use the structural enum, not serialization strings or the more restrictive C1 raw-Ward token.

Native ordinary `ProvedZero` after setting q^2=0 can independently support the scalar null/scaleless classification but cannot replace the theorem. For C_n>1, off-shell q^2 mass jets precede restriction to the cone; setting F(0)=0 before differentiating can lose contact terms. Even C1 needs the common-regulator restriction theorem to identify ordinary DR zero with the thermal cut product. Polynomial medium tensors are permitted by the germ theorem because Gaussian tensor powers add nonnegative c; inverse energies, massive lines, external-only denominators and multiple occupied cuts are outside this singleton implication.

This assessment needs independent review of the uniform Taylor/Holder remainder and regulated mass-jet order before implementation. It proposes no code changes. The finite-positive-eta origin prototype, infinity boundary owner, and physical singleton-zero optimization remain distinct capabilities.

## Concrete proposed consumer and exact checks

A separate private `PartialEndpointEvidence` could require the fully replayed `PartialOriginCapability`, retain its complete routing/support/channel/chart identity, and add a distinct endpoint theorem version. It would compute per-label P, P0, R, J_i, the fixed N and every virtual/angular/radial/lower margin above. It must audit the union of actual targets, retained candidates and final basis labels, as well as the final rationally reconstructed target. No constructor may infer endpoint authority from an origin identity string alone. Existing higher-rank/all-uncut permits would remain unchanged.

The right-half-plane contour requirement also remains explicit. The same estimates hold for Re(eta)>=0 with rho using |eta|: Re(eta*S)>=0 and |c^2*h+eta+regulators|>=c^2*h. No negative-real auxiliary path is certified. The exact native ODE and shared regular-singular endpoint owner remain responsible for existence and sufficient order of the Frobenius series, coefficients/conditions, joint target combination and numerical divergence checks; the theorem supplies the physical dimensional-continuation interpretation rather than replacing those checks.

`check.py` supplies **384 exact finite-index margin witnesses**, including raised cuts, lower/upper contacts, polynomial degrees and puretransfer powers. It also reads the frozen native full-polynomial records and checks **117 eta=0 parameter-face/angle points**, including **113 with F0=0**. These are regression checks of the inequalities/algebra, not a fresh native reconstruction, numerical integration, or proof of the analytic limit. `checks.json` records every witness and input hash.
