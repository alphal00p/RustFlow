This is a read-only review of the frozen generic virtual-soft draft. No production file, frozen assessment artifact, native program, or numerical result was changed or executed. The proposed helper is not currently called by boundary evaluation.

The algebraic zero classification is conservative. On a full virtual-rank support it reconstructs each retained denominator as an exact rational multiple of one momentum square plus a residual, solves one common affine virtual translation, rejects every nonzero residual, and verifies that translated denominators contain no compact or medium coordinates. The complete numerator is translated and remains polynomial. On a deficient-rank support, a free ordinary virtual direction remains polynomial even when other directions carry masses or external scales. Both cases still require an actual native unrestricted `ProvedZero` decision for every exact ordinary term. Thus the rank test is not itself treated as a replacement for the native proof. No nonzero massive vacuum or external bubble is accepted merely because its expression is homogeneous in some coordinates.

The reconstruction sign is consistent: solving A*T=C and substituting old k=new k-T*q removes the external part. For (k-q)^2, T=-1 and old k=new k+q. This is a triangular unit-Jacobian translation within the ordinary virtual integration space. It does not change a compact shell, theta factor, physical mass derivative, or the region Jacobian. Its use under actual complex shell/Feynman regulators still belongs to the separately sealed common-continuation proof.

`ProjectedRegion` semantics match the intended call site. Its hard and soft terms are already scalar-separated after hard tensor projection. `RegionFactorSpace` lists local Gram coordinates in the selected loop order, then loop–external coordinates; the draft reconstructs the ordinary coordinates in the same order. Its source loop indices refer to the transformed region routing. `cut_regions::adapt_compact_region` fixes each occupied coordinate exactly, so matching distributions by source loop index is valid for this owner. Region Jacobians, hard coefficients, dimensions, complete distribution coverage and placement admission remain caller responsibilities; the certificate is only for one exact soft factor.

Two integration requirements are blockers before this becomes authority to skip a boundary term:

- The frozen draft certificate and nested proof fields are mutable `pub` data. A caller could replace the original expression or region map while retaining a valid native proof for a different factor. The revised draft owner is already changing these fields to private, immutable accessors. The eventual consuming API should verify the exact expression and full region-space identity, plus the placement/origin proof identity, before allowing zero. Merely possessing a native certificate for some ordinary family is insufficient.
- Construction only retains nonzero conditions; it does not validate a physical epsilon/parameter assignment. A future caller must check every returned condition and preserve incoming target, reduction, hard-coefficient and region conditions before skipping a term. Conditions involving compact integration coordinates are correctly refused. A guard failure must remain an explicit failure, not become a successful zero or a fallback to homogeneity. The caller must retain the separate common high-D compact-origin proof; distribution index validation alone does not admit a massless mu=0 endpoint, a singular lower contact, or a partial mass placement.

The raw-pole claim has a precise boundary. `parameter_conditions` inspects the expression it receives before its own together/cancel step and retains raw parameter poles while refusing raw compact-only poles. However `projected_factor_region` already applies together/factor to its incoming expression before producing `term.soft`. A denominator erased upstream cannot be recovered by this helper. Therefore the final contract must either prove that the admitted region expansion introduces no such compact poles, carry upstream denominator-domain evidence, or reject them before projection. The helper's local pre-cancellation check must not be advertised as preserving all original input syntax. The current tested partial masks shift pure compact transfers, whose large-eta expansions are polynomial, so this is chiefly a binding/coverage requirement for a generic future caller.

The native term conversion and zero decision retain appropriate ownership: `integrand::to_integrals` owns exact partial fractions and completion; ordinary `family.convert` and the native zero analyzer own period-zero evidence. A failed native decision returns Unsupported. Reverse-mapped native conditions and raw coefficient denominators remain in the result. No custom eliminator, hand-inserted period value, inverse Gram assumption, or Kira dependency appears in this draft.

The five uncompiled tests cover useful distinct paths: translated numerator and a removable parameter pole, compact/external/mass rejections, one free direction, distribution validation before missing-cut handling, and actual generated regions for three masks plus two discovered singleton labels. They do not yet establish production admission or boundary numerical matching. Strengthen them before integration with:

1. Noncontiguous source loop indices and reordered local virtual/compact positions; at least two compact loops, raised cuts, and positive upper/lower indices. Include exact coordinate substitution reconstruction and unchanged compact coordinates.
2. A null direction that is a nontrivial combination of two virtual coordinates, with a massive remaining denominator, plus a genuinely massive full-rank vacuum that must fail.
3. Native proof nonzero conditions that survive conversion, parameter specialization violating them, and attempted certificate reuse with a changed expression, map, placement or distribution binding. Test private-field/getter mutation rejection once capability sealing is implemented.
4. An upstream removable compact pole and a removed parameter pole passed through `projected_factor_region`, demonstrating the actual preservation boundary. Do not count a direct-helper test as an end-to-end original-domain test.
5. Multiple separated products where one virtual factor is proved zero but another retains a nonzero scale or has no native proof. Only that certified term may be skipped; failure must not certify the entire region.
6. Zero budgets, denominator/term-budget exhaustion, malformed coordinate maps and invalid distributions. `source_coordinate_indices` should at least have matching length and unique entries, while any mapping to the parent family must be checked by the consuming owner.

No presently reachable production unsoundness is identified: the draft is unintegrated, and current occupied boundaries reject the partial masks and nonpolynomial virtual soft factors. Subject to the integration requirements above, no additional mathematical blocker was found in the narrow factor-zero classifier. Compilation and actual generated-region tests remain necessary and are being performed separately by its owner; this review does not claim they passed.
