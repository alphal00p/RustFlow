This is an additional certificate algorithm proposal, not an implemented owner or a completed common-regulator theorem. It strengthens the parameter-chart portion of design.md without changing its admission conclusion.

For a full-rank active virtual support of n rows a_e in Q^h, Cauchy–Binet gives U=det(sum alpha_e a_e a_e^T)=sum_B det(A_B)^2 product_(e in B) alpha_e. The sum runs over independent h-row bases; every nonzero coefficient is positive. No period is evaluated.

Partition the positive projective simplex into the n! ordered primary charts. In a permutation pi, use max gauge alpha_(pi0)=1 and alpha_(pij)=t1...tj for j>=1, with every t in [0,1]. Scanning that row order, greedily retain a row precisely when it increases exact rational rank. The chosen h-row basis G contains rank(prefix j) members of every prefix. Every other basis contains at most rank(prefix j) members. Consequently the exponent of each t_j in alpha_G, which is h minus that prefix intersection size, is no greater than its exponent in alpha_B. Thus alpha_G divides every monomial of U on the chart. The quotient U0 is polynomial with nonnegative coefficients and has the nonzero constant det(A_G)^2. It is a positive analytic unit on the entire closed cube.

If at least one shifted virtual row is active, let e* be the first such row in the ordering. Its alpha monomial divides every shifted alpha. Therefore S=sum_(shifted active virtual e) alpha_e also equals a monomial times a positive polynomial unit S0>=1.

Let V_ij=U*C_ij-B_i^T adj(A) B_j. After the exact affine placement witness, the Schur identity implies |V_ij|/(U*S)<=K_ij on the real positive chart interior. Substitute the chart, collect V_ij exactly, and divide by the already proved monomial factor of U*S. If any collected coefficient had a negative valuation in one chart variable, fix the other variables to generic positive values: the first nonzero coefficient in that variable would diverge as the variable tends to zero. That contradicts the uniform Schur bound. Hence the quotient numerator has nonnegative valuations in every variable. Dividing by U0*S0 now gives a rational function analytic with bounded derivatives on the closed cube. A replay owner should check these valuations directly, rather than trusting the boundedness argument or an approximate sample. Diagonal V_ii also has a bordered-Gram positive expansion; cross coefficients may have signs and must be collected before valuation checks.

A bounded exact certificate can retain the complete chart permutations, selected greedy row basis, prefix ranks, nonzero determinant, U and S monomial valuations, positive-unit polynomials, all external coefficient numerators/valuations, and their exact identities against the native generated Gaussian polynomials. The charts cover the full projective domain up to measure-zero ordering boundaries, with an explicit Jacobian. Support count, chart count, polynomial size, and arithmetic work require explicit limits; exhaustion grants no evidence. This uses the actual complete active virtual row inventory, including every unshifted denominator. Nonpositive indices are polynomial insertions and do not add Schwinger parameters.

At zero line regulators, this supplies precisely the smooth external-invariant ratios in F=U*S*(eta+sum z_ij R_ij) needed for a small fixed-eta germ after finite parameter-face meromorphic continuation. Tensor numerators have finite polynomial/adjugate factors and extra U powers, so their finite labels require stronger exponents and Taylor orders, not new inverse external Gram determinants. This chart result does not alone assert a convergent unregulated integral; in particular, full-rank supports may contain factorized scaleless subintegrals even when a shifted row is present. Their analytic-index/UV–IR treatment must remain explicit.

The remaining regulator issue is real. Independent line masses contribute M_reg=sum alpha_e epsilon_e, including unshifted rows. M_reg/S may be unbounded at faces where shifted parameters vanish. Smoothness of V/(U*S) therefore does not by itself justify removing those independent regulators after UV continuation, nor does a single high-D inequality automatically dominate every nested UV Taylor subtraction at arbitrary loop rank. The saved small-support proof handles its tadpole and connected two-loop vacuum charts explicitly. A general owner needs a separately justified uniform finite-jet continuation theorem or additional exact support/face evidence for this limit, with the established fixed-T Fermi strip and ordering retained.

No eta-zero endpoint, rational-soft infinity boundary, native rule, or numerical amplitude is certified by this proposal. It should be reviewed separately before entering a sealed capability.
