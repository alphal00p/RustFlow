# Certificate preflight, before physical fitting

The bounded recurrence/theta fitter is implemented and synthetically tested. A generic physical creative-telescoping discovery/verifier adapter is **not implemented**. The current producer emits source-bound exact integer-n Gaussian/compact coefficients, not a machine-readable symbolic-n hyperexponential integrand or finite-sum certificate contract. Therefore the certificate phase currently has status `unsupported_input`, not `proved` or `searched_and_failed`. No certificate runtime has been consumed and no physical fit has begun.

This is an interface/method limitation, not a claim that the sequence lacks an all-n definition or that a low-order identity cannot exist. The coefficient algorithm supplies an exact constructive family at each admitted integer n. Proving a fitted recurrence still needs an identity covering all n and its initial/singular indices. A source-bound convergent germ licenses interpreting an all-n coefficient identity as a function identity locally; it does not promote a finite fit to that identity.

A minimal generic telescoping input would supply the exact scalar term T(n,x), rational shift quotients T(n+j,x)/T(n,x), rational integration logarithmic derivatives, all integration domains and every boundary functional, normalization/parameter conditions and a proof that this representation equals the complete requested integral. Multiple leaves or finite sums require their own exact composition rules. Merely providing a polynomial U/F does not discharge mass-derivative/Fermi boundary terms. The producer confirms that a generic parent representation exists in principle for this case with at most four variables (Schwinger simplex coordinate, two compact radial variables, one angle), but generation/admission of its C2 bulk/surface leaves is additional work. No special evaluated single-invariant formula is substituted for that missing owner.

If that interface becomes available, the predeclared search remains <=4 variables; denominators from <=8 existing factors with powers<=2 and total degree<=12; numerator integration degree<=3 and n degree<=2; <=420 unknowns/2048 equations; <=300s;200000 retained monomials/128MiB canonical payload, excluding CAS scratch. A prospective exact identity is sum_j p_j(n) T(n+j)/T(n)=sum_i(d_i R_i+R_i*d_i logT). Verification is Symbolica exact polynomial equality after declared denominator clearing. All boundaries must vanish by proof or remain explicit forcing. Absence of an input contract does not authorize increasing those bounds.

A heldout pass may justify a conditional guessed-equation numerical diagnostic. It remains distinct from a physical prediction/certificate. Existing ordinary DifferentialSystem::compile/CompiledSystem::transport can transport a rational companion supplied as a candidate, without installing a fake ordinary-integral family or restarting weighted IBP.

For direct theta L=sum_j a_j(t)theta^j, states (g,theta g,...), theta=t*d/dt, give the rational companion away from a_r=0. For recurrence cores, L=sum_j t^(r-j)p_j(theta-j) has startup forcing B(t); restoring a branch t^beta conjugates theta to theta-beta and yields forcing t^beta B(t). One auxiliary state h=t^beta, theta h=beta h, retains this forcing without discarding it or automatically increasing the derivative order by r. For eta=1/t, the transport matrix is -C(1/eta)/eta. Distinct normalization/exponent channels retain separate exact constants and may require separate companion blocks. No companion/conversion implementation or physical transport has yet been run.

Candidate transport would still require explicit branches, candidate leading-coefficient exclusions, initial series error, singular endpoint handling, precision/order/starting-point controls and exact metadata binding. The existing numerical ODE error controls certify numerical integration of the supplied candidate equation, not that the physical period obeys it. Any unavailable physical initial-tail bound must be labeled as a missing bound, not replaced by observed coefficient ratios or two numerical runs agreeing. Fixed-D evidence cannot certify symbolic epsilon/Laurent continuation.
