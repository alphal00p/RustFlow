This is a read-only design assessment. Existing schema-1 checkpoints are diagnostic evidence, not a supported resume format. The separate longer singleton run starts fresh.

The current owner preserves a native program bound to the complete source context, its hash and byte count, current frontier, historical/submitted/completed/deferred requests, next-needed labels and the linked requested-discovery transaction. Native decode can replay that program against freshly reconstructed sources. These are useful ingredients, but they do not serialize the whole algorithm state.

The material gaps are:

- `reduction/active.rs` accumulates nonzero conditions from every recursive target/derivative reduction, including successful intermediate steps whose coefficients later cancel. That map is not in the checkpoint. Transaction records contain direct probe conditions, which are only part of the map. `retired-unresolved.json` appears only after closure and records unresolved rows, not the accumulated conditions.
- Direct-zero completed-point memo, call boundary and cumulative diagnostic counters are not attached to the round manifest. Separate direct-zero files can include work from an unpublished next attempt. A completed search is neither a zero nor a coverage certificate.
- Provisional metadata is published before the owner checks application failures/frontier overflow and before final closure auditing. Its presence does not establish a safe next-round phase. The mutable request-history file is not a substitute for the program-bound round state.
- The round metadata omits a complete standalone target/deformation/options contract and legacy learned/refinement state. Input/configuration files and source identity help external auditing, but the native program alone is not that contract.

For these timeout runs, each corpus has one additional transaction marked `complete` beyond the latest published provisional round. It documents completed discovery, not a completed active-closure round or a recoverable retained program. No new round, frontier or conditions may be inferred from it.

A minimal future owner should introduce a typed active-closure state and versioned resumable manifest, initially limited to the requested-ray-point schedule. Persist the original weighted targets and raw coefficient guards; exact deformation, parameter map, physical arity, storage tail and admitted domain; all accumulated conditions; history/next-needed/deferred sets; direct-zero memo/counters; historical gaps; and cumulative round/resource diagnostics. Bind the complete source/policy/physical-evidence identity and every program/transaction/zero sidecar hash. Atom serialization must preserve exact variables through the native context rather than trusting an unbound printed expression.

Publish a generation manifest atomically only after the reachability and failure/limit checks. State explicitly whether the next action is another round or the final one-program target/derivative audit. Resume must decode/replay into freshly reconstructed sources, verify all identities/options/targets, retain old conditions, preserve request/rule/round limits and never infer coverage from history. Legacy refinement would additionally need its learned domains and search state; it should not inherit the narrow format automatically.

Required rejection tests include changed targets or raw coefficient poles, deformation/source/zero-domain/role changes, a nonzero storage tail, stale terminals, mismatched or partial sidecars, unknown symbols, dropped conditions, changed budgets without an explicit new-run policy, inconsistent history/memo counters, and old schema-1 metadata. Split/uninterrupted runs should produce identical final target/derivative maps and retained conditions, including a condition from a term that later cancels and a conditional exceptional point. Cancellation at each publication phase must fail safely.

A separately named audited standalone continuation may be possible from the historical evidence, but it would not make these checkpoints natively resumable. After the parent process ends, bind all historical programs/transactions and original input. Reconstruct exact targets and source context, replay every original target and every historical frontier derivative under its corresponding program, and retain a conservative union of all resulting conditions, saved successful-probe conditions and original raw coefficient guards. Recompute the latest reachability, failures and deferred obligations before advancing. Extra guards may restrict the parameter domain; never drop a guard merely because it excludes the requested epsilon. Such a superset is not exact restoration of the former condition history.

The direct-zero memo must either be independently re-proved from an authenticated sidecar prefix or explicitly reset and researched with changed scheduling/cost semantics. Unpublished files cannot silently become committed state. Preserve original elapsed cost and completed-round limits, and label repeated work. A final claimed connection still requires the ordinary native one-program audit. This proposal is unimplemented and untested; no resume or amplitude success is claimed here.
